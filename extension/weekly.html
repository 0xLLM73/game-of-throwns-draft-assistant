Projection format and scoring

Use an authorized provider’s weekly half-PPR total, or expected stats from weekly player props. Season totals and ESPN projections are not weekly Vegas projections. Use the source’s actual update time.

Passing: yards ÷ 25 + TDs × 4 − interceptions × 2. Rushing/receiving: yards ÷ 10 + TDs × 6 + receptions × 0.5. Subtract lost fumbles × 2 and add 2-point conversions × 2 when provided.

Prop lines are estimates, not exact expected outcomes. An anytime-TD probability is not an expected TD count. Missing required props show “Incomplete” rather than a misleading total. K and D/ST need a direct projection matching your league’s custom scoring.
